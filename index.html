    Study smarter 🌱

    A few methods worth trying.

    🧠

    Active Recall

    Close your notes and try to explain what you remember. Check your notes afterwards.

    🔁

    Spaced Revision

    Come back to important topics over several days instead of trying to remember everything at once.

    🪴

    Start Small

    Replace “I have so much to study” with one clear next task. Starting is the hard part.

    📱

    Phone Boundaries

    Keep your phone out of reach while you work, then check it during a planned break.
